1. Information We Collect

When you create a Nerum account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely encrypted version of your password. We never store or have access to your actual plain-text password — it is immediately converted into an unreadable encrypted format (using bcrypt hashing) before being stored. If you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive only your name and email address from Google, and no password is stored on our end at all.

As you use Nerum, we collect profile information you provide such as your business name, phone number, preferred language, and plan type. We also store the automation workflows you create, including their triggers, actions, configuration settings, and execution history. This is necessary to run your automations on your behalf.

When you connect third-party services like WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, or Google Sheets to Nerum, you may provide API keys or access tokens. These credentials are stored in an encrypted form and are used only to execute the automations you configure. We also collect usage data automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages you visit on our platform, actions you take, and your login history. This helps us keep the platform secure and improve your experience.

Payment information such as your billing details and transaction history is processed directly and securely by Razorpay, our payment partner. We do not store your credit card or bank account numbers on our servers at any point. We only retain a transaction reference and subscription status for billing management purposes.

If you contact our support team, we retain those communications to help resolve your issue and improve our service quality.

2. How We Use Your Information

The primary reason we collect your information is to provide you with the Nerum Service — to run your automation workflows, manage your account, process your subscription, and deliver the features you signed up for. Without this data, we cannot operate the platform on your behalf.

We use your email address to send you important transactional communications such as account verification emails, one-time passwords (OTP) for two-factor authentication, password reset links, and billing notifications. These are essential service communications and cannot be opted out of while you hold an active account.

We analyze usage patterns and platform behavior in an aggregated, anonymized way to understand how our features are being used, identify bugs, and improve the overall product experience. This analysis does not involve reviewing your personal automations or messages in any individually identifiable way.

We also use your information to detect and prevent fraudulent activity, unauthorized access, abuse, and spam. Our security systems monitor login patterns and usage behavior to protect your account and our platform. If we detect suspicious activity, we may take action including temporarily locking your account and notifying you immediately.

Occasionally, we may send you product updates, feature announcements, or tips to help you get more out of Nerum. You can opt out of these marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any such email or by contacting us at support@nerum.in.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on several legal grounds depending on the context. The primary basis is contractual necessity — we need to process your data in order to fulfill our obligations to you under the Terms of Service and deliver the Service you signed up for. This includes running your workflows, managing your subscription, and sending you transactional emails.

We also process certain data based on our legitimate business interests, such as monitoring platform security, preventing fraud, analyzing product usage in anonymized form, and improving our services. In all such cases, we ensure that our interests do not override your privacy rights.

Where we send you marketing communications, we do so based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. And where applicable law requires us to retain or share certain data, we process it to comply with our legal obligations.

4. Data Storage and Security

All your data is stored on Supabase, a secure PostgreSQL database platform hosted on industry-grade cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. All communication between your browser and our servers is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption, which means your data cannot be read by anyone intercepting the connection.

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is run through the bcrypt hashing algorithm, which is a one-way process — meaning even our own engineers cannot see or recover your actual password. Your account is also protected by JWT-based authentication tokens that expire after a defined period, requiring you to re-authenticate for continued access.

To protect against unauthorized access, we automatically lock your account after a set number of consecutive failed login attempts and send you an alert. We also offer two-factor authentication (2FA) via Email OTP, which we strongly recommend enabling. All API keys and third-party credentials you store in Nerum are encrypted before being saved to the database.

While we take all reasonable and industry-standard steps to protect your data, it is important to acknowledge that no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we commit to promptly notifying you and taking corrective action in the event of a security incident affecting your data.

5. Third-Party Services

Nerum integrates with a number of third-party services in order to deliver its features. These include Meta's WhatsApp Business API for sending and receiving WhatsApp messages, Resend for delivering transactional emails, Google for OAuth-based login and Google Sheets and Forms integration, Telegram for bot-based messaging, Razorpay for processing subscription payments, Anthropic's Claude AI for powering AI features, Supabase for database hosting, and Render for application hosting.

Each of these third-party providers has their own independently maintained privacy policy that governs how they collect and use data in connection with their services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these providers. We encourage you to review their policies directly. We carefully select our service providers and only work with those who maintain high standards of data security and privacy.

Importantly, we have confirmed with our AI provider (Anthropic) that Google Workspace APIs and user data are not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models without your explicit consent.

6. API Keys and Credentials

When you connect your WhatsApp Business account, Gmail, Telegram bot, or any other external service to Nerum, you may be required to provide API keys, access tokens, or other credentials. We treat these credentials with the highest level of sensitivity. They are encrypted using strong encryption algorithms before being stored in our database, and they are never transmitted or exposed in plain text.

These credentials are used solely and exclusively to execute the automation workflows you have configured. We do not use them to access your connected accounts for any other purpose, and we do not share them with any other users, third parties, or external systems. You can revoke or delete any stored credentials at any time directly from your Nerum account settings, and we will promptly remove them from our systems.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Nerum uses a minimal set of cookies that are strictly necessary to operate the platform. Specifically, we use session cookies that keep you logged in while you use the Service, and security cookies that help us detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity. These cookies do not track you across other websites and are not used for advertising purposes of any kind.

We do not use any third-party analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking technologies that monitor your activity outside of Nerum. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, but please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of the platform, including staying logged in.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to any third party under any circumstances. Your data is yours, and we treat it that way. We share your data only in the specific and limited circumstances described here.

We share data with the service providers listed in Section 5 above, but only to the extent necessary for them to perform their function in delivering the Nerum Service to you. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

We may disclose your information if we are legally required to do so — for example, in response to a valid court order, subpoena, government investigation, or regulatory requirement. In such cases, we will disclose only the minimum information required to comply with the legal obligation, and we will notify you in advance wherever permitted by law.

If Nerum is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. In such an event, we will provide you with advance notice via email or a prominent platform notice, and the receiving party will be bound by privacy standards no less protective than this policy.

We may also share information when we reasonably believe it is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nerum, our users, or the public — for example, to investigate a suspected fraud or security breach.

9. Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may collect, analyze, and use data about how our platform is used in an aggregated and anonymized form — meaning it cannot be linked back to any individual user. For example, we may track how many workflows were executed on a given day, which integrations are most popular, or what features users engage with most. We may use this information to improve our platform, develop new features, and occasionally publish general insights about automation trends. This aggregated data contains no personally identifiable information.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service to you. If you choose to delete your account, we will delete your personal account information and workflow history within 30 days of receiving your deletion request. Encrypted backup copies of data are fully purged within 90 days.

There are some limited exceptions where we may retain certain data for longer periods. For example, payment records and transaction histories may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations in India. In such cases, we retain only the minimum data required by law, and it is not used for any other purpose.

11. Your Rights

You have meaningful rights over your personal data, and we are committed to honoring them. You have the right to access a copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time, and to request correction of any information that is inaccurate or incomplete. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data, subject to the legal retention exceptions described above.

You also have the right to data portability — meaning you can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format. You may object to certain types of processing, such as processing based on legitimate interests, and request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances while a dispute is being resolved.

If you have provided consent for any specific type of processing — such as receiving marketing emails — you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@nerum.in. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

12. Children's Privacy

Nerum is a business automation platform intended exclusively for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we discover or are made aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our systems. If you believe that a minor has provided us with their personal information, please contact us immediately at support@nerum.in so we can investigate and act accordingly.

14. Security Incidents

Our notification will describe the nature of the incident, the categories of data affected, the steps we have taken to address the breach, and any actions you can take to protect yourself. We maintain an incident response plan and conduct regular security assessments to minimize the risk of such events.
