Data Deletion Request

Last updated: May 17, 2025

At Nerum, you have full control over your data. If you would like to request deletion of your personal data and account from our platform, this page explains exactly how to do it and what happens after your request is received.

1. How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion in two ways:

Way 1 — Email us at support@nerum.in with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days and send confirmation.

Way 2 — Go to Settings inside your Nerum dashboard and click "Delete Account". This immediately triggers the deletion process.

2. What Gets Deleted

  • Your name, email address and account profile
  • All workflows you created
  • All workflow run history and logs
  • Smart Lists and all associated records
  • Connected API keys and integration credentials
  • Login history and session data
  • Support communications

3. What We Are Required to Retain

Certain data must be retained to comply with Indian law:

  • Payment records and transaction history (7 years — Income Tax Act)
  • Anonymized, non-identifiable usage statistics (no personal info)

4. Timeline

We will complete your deletion request within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete. Encrypted backup copies are fully purged within 90 days.
