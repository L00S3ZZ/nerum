Connect WhatsApp Business
You'll need three values from your Meta for Developers app. Follow these steps to grab them, then continue.
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and open your WhatsApp Business app (or create one).
  2. Under WhatsApp → API Setup, copy your Phone Number ID and WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID).
  3. Generate a permanent Access Token via System Users in Business Settings (temporary tokens expire after 24 hours).
  4. Make sure your phone number is verified and a recipient is added for testing.
