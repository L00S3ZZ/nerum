WhatsApp Business Setup Guide

Complete guide to connect and verify your WhatsApp Business account

⏱ 15-30 mins setup  •  7-15 days verification

1
Create Your Meta Business App
Set up a Meta developer app to access WhatsApp Cloud API
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and sign in with your Meta account
  2. Click Create App
  3. Select Business as the app type
  4. Enter your app name — e.g. "YourBusiness WA"
  5. Select your Meta Business Account from the dropdown
  6. Click Create App to confirm
💡 Tip: Use your business name in the app name for easy identification later
2
Add WhatsApp to Your App
Enable WhatsApp Cloud API as a product in your Meta app
  1. Inside your app dashboard, scroll to the Add Products to Your App section
  2. Find WhatsApp and click Set Up
  3. Select your Business Portfolio when prompted
  4. Click Continue
ℹ️ This links WhatsApp Cloud API to your Meta app — required to send and receive messages programmatically
3
Get Your Credentials
Copy the three values you'll paste into Nerum
  1. Go to WhatsApp → API Setup in the left sidebar of your app
  2. Copy your Phone Number ID — paste this into Nerum
  3. Copy your WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID) — paste this into Nerum
  4. For the Access Token: go to Business Settings → System Users
  5. Create a System User (select Admin role)
  6. Click Generate New Token → select your app → check whatsapp_business_messaging permission
  7. Copy the token and paste into Nerum
⚠️ Use a PERMANENT token from System Users, NOT the temporary token. Temporary tokens expire in 24 hours!
Go to API Setup →
4
Meta Business Verification
Required for full messaging access and higher rate limits
Required for full messaging access
📅 Timeline: 7-15 business days after submission
What you need
  • Business Registration Certificate / GST Certificate
  • Business website (must match business name)
  • Business email — not Gmail/Yahoo, must be domain email (e.g. you@yourbusiness.com)
  • Business phone number
  • Utility bill or bank statement (address proof)
  1. Go to business.facebook.com
  2. Click Settings → Business Info
  3. Scroll to the Business Verification section
  4. Click Start Verification
  5. Select your country (India)
  6. Enter your business legal name — must match your documents exactly
  7. Upload the required documents
  8. Submit and wait for Meta review (7-15 business days)
❌ Common rejection reasons:
  • Business name doesn't match documents exactly
  • Website not live or doesn't mention your business
  • Using personal Gmail instead of domain email
  • Documents not in English (get translated copies)
Go to Meta Business Manager →
